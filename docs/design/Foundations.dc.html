01 — PRINCIPLES
Readable at arm's length
Packing happens standing, one-handed, in bad light. Primary state must read without bringing the phone to your face. Big targets, high contrast, bottom-reachable actions.
Never color alone
Every state = shape + fill-level + label. Empty ○ → half ◪ → full ■. Works colorblind, works in sunlight, works on a bad screen.
Two worlds, never conflated
Home and Trip are separate columns of reality. Home rows carry ⌂ + neutral chips; trip rows carry the pennant + ink chips. Same gear, two whereabouts, both visible.
The ledger is the truth
Terse, factual, numeric. Counts are load-bearing UI, set in mono with tabular figures. No cheerleading, no exclamation marks.
Depth without drowning
Containment is a path, not a maze: ATTIC ▸ SHELF ▸ CRATE B. One level on screen at a time; moving a container moves its contents in one action.
Offline is normal
No signal is not an error. Search, edit and pack fully on-device; sync state is one quiet line of metadata, never a blocking dialog.
