A5 — A CONTAINER IS NOT A PIECEBLESSED
Confirmed: sync §3.7's never both on one entry means a container Entry has no status pill anywhere — its rail is its whole state, and it cannot be "packed" in the pill's sense. Invariant 18's own shape (trip-only entries are excluded from the open count because they take no outcome). Narrows §5b L: PIECES counts what carries a status.
A6 — THE DISAGREEMENT THRESHOLDDRAWN
The rule the two examples encode: the line appears at CAR and PACKED, never at HOME or STAGING. Staging is the act of packing — the crate is on the floor precisely so things can go in it, so unpacked contents there are the work, not a contradiction. Once it is in the car the household has moved it beyond the reach of the work. Disagreeing = not packed only; counting staged would fire on nearly every container in the car and the ▲ would stop meaning anything. Counts contents at any depth.
A7 — PERSON MODEREDRAWN
PERSON mode ships, and it stops meaning whose body it goes with — that is story 23 and the app holds no such fact. Ownership partitions every Entry, so the arithmetic closes again on facts the MVP has. The frame's note is redrawn with it. Frame at 02.

Dropped, on three counts. A8 — ALL MODEDRAWN
Every Entry and Piece, flat, no group headers, name A→Z. Sorting by status would move rows under the thumb as they are tapped. Each row's meta gains its trip residence as its last segment, amber, since no header states it. Containers do not appear: ALL lists things with a status, and a container's journey lives on its header in CONTAINER mode. Frame at 02.

WHY A GROUP AND NOT A VARIANT
COUNT's subject is a quantity that splits — owned-count less the bring-counts claimed. Per-person gear has no owned-count at all (invariant 6), so there is no quantity to split and its whole answer is which People. Two units, two groups, and each group's hint names its unit. One chip per Person: a 22px circle (chrome-cluster density — a chip riding a card is chrome) plus that Person's own whereabouts string, B1's rule at chip density.
ONE RULE, TWO SURFACES (B3 · B5)
A per-Person breakdown renders only when the answers can differ. So PIECES appears only while a Piece is on an active trip, and Find's card only then too — otherwise three rows draw one home path three times, which is 02C's identical-circles argument and the S5 border rule's own case. Off a trip, gear detail's whereabouts card and Find's plain 2-line row each say it once.
B5 — A REMOVED PIECE READS AS HOME
Kees is not bringing one — trip.piece_removed — and his row reads ⌂ HAL ▸ LADE 2, because that is where it is. The tombstone is a fact about a trip; Find asks about gear, and the card never mentions the removal. A row reading NOT BRINGING ONE would draw exactly the status invariant 11 forbids.
THE CARD'S ONE ADDITION
A Piece belongs to at most one active trip, so a piece row never names two — except where the over-claim the model guards but never prevents has arrived through sync. Then the row takes the unaccounted row's anatomy exactly:

WHY STATUS IS NOT A DEPOT SLICE
Rule 1 first: a chip reading STATUS: PACKED on the Depot's slice bar shows packing status on the Depot, which is what retired the Pieces rung. And the NOT IN ANY TRIP sentinel is not the same shape: TRIP answers membership, a property of the list; status answers how far along a pack-out is, which has no value at all for gear on no trip. A dimension undefined for four-fifths of the list is a filter for a different list.

To F4, whose segmented control and ○ LEFT pill already are a group-and-filter control in everything but name, and which is the only list where packed has a value for every row.
