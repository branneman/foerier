A small round: one drawing question and four that fall out of it. S9a shipped with one recorded hole — no shipped control writes a per-person Entry's own trip residence, so CONTAINER and ALL state different places for the same gear. The maintainer has since ruled the product question: for per-person gear, where it is is only ever a per-Piece fact. Nothing sits above the Pieces. That kills one of the README's two recorded candidates before this round starts; what is left is where the row goes. No new op types, no endpoints, no migration — everything here is read-side, and S9b reads it.

C0 — THE WHOLE-ENTRY MOVERETIRED
Not ruled here — marked. README §1's first recorded candidate, the Piece status sheet grows a whole-Entry MOVE beside its per-Piece one, is a set-level residence, and the maintainer's ruling says that fact does not exist for per-person gear. Its code consequence needs no drawing: trip.entry_moved on a per-person Entry becomes fold-but-ignore — a reader gate, the shape a bring-count already has on non-Counted gear — and a Piece with no residence of its own reads loose, never its Entry's. Retired before it was drawn.
C1 — WHERE THE ROW GOESDRAWN
One row per group holding at least one of its Pieces, the cluster and its count scoped to that group. A container group's whole job is to be truthful about its contents. The single row under a majority (shape 2) makes the header count Pieces that are not in it, and has no majority at all at one-one-one — consensus is a set-level residence by another name, the fact the ruling says does not exist. Shape 3's Piece rows are what C1 draws when every Piece is apart; today's one clustered row is what it draws when they are together. C1 is the rule of which both are cases, so no drawn frame changes. Frame at 01.
C1b — THE FOURTH SHAPE, REFUSEDDRAWN
§5d E's raw material — the full cluster with the Pieces not here drawn dashed and dim — was considered and refused. Dashed + dim is PersonCluster's word for excluded on the builder; a removed Piece is not drawn on F4 at all; so a dashed K on a packing row would read Kees isn't bringing one, the exact state invariant 11 expresses by removal. One tone meaning two things across two callers of one primitive is what §5d J's attach-the-rule-to-the-primitive forbids. The cluster paints what is here. A one-circle cluster among three-circle rows is itself the signal; C2 names it.
C2 — THE META COUNTREDRAWN
Scoped to the group, and it names the rest: PER-PERSON · 1/1 · 2 ELSEWHERE under the duffel, PER-PERSON · 0/1 · 2 ELSEWHERE under Loose. Scoped because a row's count must sum into the header above it — an Entry-wide 1/3 under a header counting one of the three is the row-disagrees-with-header symptom the shared arithmetic exists to prevent. N ELSEWHERE appears only at N > 0, so PER-PERSON · 1/3 stands on every drawn frame with its meaning narrowed: packed over Pieces in this group. Muted, not amber — a remainder, not a residence; the residence is the header, and a set in two bags is not a fault.
C3 — WHAT THE SHEET LISTSBLESSED
All of the Entry's Pieces. The sheet is about the Entry — the title is the gear name, the fact line is Entry-wide, SET EVERYONE means everyone — and each of its rows already names that Piece's residence, so it is the one surface where the split is seen whole and mended at MOVE. Every row of one Entry opens the same sheet: one sheet per Entry, never one per row. Cost: a status tap in the sheet may move a count under a header elsewhere on the screen. A2b's confirm is not owed — the sheet shows the change in place, and a status tap has never confirmed. Frame at 02.
C4 — UNDER LOOSEBLESSED
Yes, intended. An Entry with one Piece in the duffel and two unplaced draws under Loose too. Loose does not mean undecided: its own meta says NOT IN A CONTAINER, and invariant 1 calls loose a valid state, not a missing one — a Piece with no residence is not in a container, whatever has been decided about its siblings. Hiding unplaced Pieces from Loose would make its header lie about its contents, which is the fault this round exists to remove.
C5 — THE HEADER'S 9/12BLESSED
Pieces, at any depth, apportioned. A header counts the Pieces in it, never the Entries whose Pieces are; a per-person Entry spanning two groups contributes to both, each Piece counted once at its own place. That is a partition, not the nested double-count — nesting counts a Piece once in its own header and again in each ancestor's — so top-level groups plus Loose sum to the trip total exactly, and the total does not move when a Piece changes bags. Cheap: the derivation already yields one item per included Piece carrying its own residence; containerTotals groups by that instead of by the Entry's holder.
NOT QUESTIONS — BUILD AS DRAWN
ALL mode and ▸ MIXED stand (already per-Piece). PERSON mode stands (A7, never a row of its own). Circles are never individual targets; the cluster + its count stays one control at 34 (§5d B, §5e A1). Loose last (A3); a container is not a piece (A5); the row's two targets stand (A2) — a per-person row's body is the sheet, in every row C1 draws.

NOTHING NEW ON THE SHEET
The S9 sheet already drew three residences on three rows — this frame differs only in the names on them. It is the one surface that states the whole Entry: three Pieces, three places, three statuses, and the MOVE that mends any of them. That is why C3 lists all of them from any of the Entry's rows: scoping the sheet to one container would hide the very row the reader opened it to find, and would make SET EVERYONE mean everyone here, a narrower thing than its word.
WHAT C0 REMOVES FROM IT
Nothing visible. The candidate whole-Entry MOVE was never drawn, so nothing retires from the frame. In code, a Piece's residence read stops layering over its Entry's: an absent Piece residence reads loose, and ▸ LOOSE on Kees's row means exactly that. §11.4's a Piece cannot be pinned against a later Entry move becomes moot — there is no Entry move to pin against.

04 — WHERE THIS ROUND CONTRADICTS A SHIPPED BOARD OR DOC, PLAINLY
SPEC §11.2 · TWO DECISIONS
Two code-taken decisions are overturned by C0 and C5: a Piece with no residence reads its Entry's, then loose (now: loose), and containerTotals groups by the Entry's holder (now: by each Piece's residence). Both were right under the assumption the maintainer has since ruled false. §11 is a dated record and is not edited; this board and README §1/§5e are where the correction lives. §11.4's pinning limitation becomes moot with them.
DOMAIN §5 · SYNC §4.4
Domain §5 says each Entry carries a trip residence; sync §4.4 lists trip.entry_moved without a Kind restriction. Under the ruling a per-person Entry's residence register is folded and never read — the same shape sync §3.7 already gives stage xor status: an authoring rule, gated on the way out. Both docs want one sentence saying so. Named, not resolved here.
