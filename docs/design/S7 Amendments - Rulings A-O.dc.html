O — THE STANDING RULE
1 · A drawn size is the painted size. The boards' h32 stepper, 32px ✕ and 22px participant circle paint at exactly those sizes.
2 · 48 floors the hit area, never the paint. The floor moves out of min-height on button/input/select — which was silently re-sizing the paint — into a non-painting hit extension on interactive controls (44 minimum, 48 target).
3 · The hit extension clamps at the owning row's bounds. Two targets never overlap; a control in a short row gets the row's full height and no more. Rows on touch surfaces are ≥56, so the clamp never bites below 44 where fingers are the pointer.
4 · Standalone controls feel nothing. Form wells, sheet buttons and full-width CTAs are drawn ≥48 already; the rule only changes controls a board deliberately drew smaller.
WHAT THIS SETTLES
The dense Stepper and EntryRow ✕ fixes are confirmed as instances of rule 1–3, not exceptions. The participant + circle (22px capsule bug, technical-debt.md, pre-S7) is fixed by the same rule: paint 22, hit clamped to its row. N is a consequence: with the paint floor gone, Start pack-out takes its drawn desk size. The touch spec's "targets ≥48" now reads "hit targets" — it always meant the tap, not the ink.
